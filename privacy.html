Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

Recast Properties LLC (“Recast Properties,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you visit our website, call us, text us, submit a form, or otherwise contact us about a property.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Property details
  • Appointment or callback preferences
  • Information you provide by phone, text message, website form, or other communication
  • Basic website usage information, such as pages visited, browser type, device information, and general location data

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your property inquiry
  • Contact you about a property you may be interested in selling
  • Schedule or confirm appointment and callback details
  • Answer questions
  • Evaluate property information
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our website, communications, and services
  • Maintain records for business and compliance purposes

Text Message Communications

If you provide your mobile number and agree to receive text messages from Recast Properties, we may send SMS messages related to your property inquiry, appointment confirmations, callback scheduling, rescheduling, and follow-up questions.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages at any time. Reply HELP for help.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of selling your property or doing business with Recast Properties.

SMS Consent and Data Sharing

Recast Properties does not sell, rent, or share SMS opt-in information or text message consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators, carriers, messaging platforms, and service providers necessary to deliver text messages and operate our SMS program.

How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business, including website hosting providers, CRM platforms, communications providers, phone and SMS providers, scheduling tools, analytics providers, and other vendors who assist with customer communication and property evaluation.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, property, users, or business operations.

Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies to understand website traffic and improve user experience. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, but some parts of the website may not function properly.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we update, correct, or delete your information, subject to legal and business recordkeeping requirements.

You may opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP.

You may contact us for help by replying HELP or by contacting us directly.
