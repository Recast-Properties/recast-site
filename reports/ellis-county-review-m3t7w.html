The market, from checkable sources

Ellis County (July 2026): median list price $489,045 (+4.8% YoY), 1,055 active listings (flat YoY but +11.3% in one month), median 53 days on market, 464 of 1,055 actives — 44% — carrying a price cut, pending ratio 43.5%. Red Oak 75154: median list $424,625, 180 actives (+7.5% YoY), 52 DOM, 49% of actives cut, $179/sqft median. Ferris 75125: median list $330,748, only 38 actives, 62 DOM, 37% cut. Meanwhile Zillow's home-value index says values are falling — 75154 down 1.9% YoY, 75125 down 0.9% — while asking prices rise. That spread is the market in one sentence: a standoff where sellers ask more, buyers pay less, and half the inventory ends up cutting. The listings that win are priced at market from day one — which, as the county's own 2026 revaluations show (all three subjects were marked down ~1% from 2025), is a slowly deflating target.

How the listing looks to a buyer

The remarks are genuinely good — the I-35 commuter pitch is the right story for Red Oak, and "some photos virtually staged" is honestly disclosed. Channel data is consistent too: recast-properties.com, MLS, and the portals all agree on 4 bd / 3.5 ba / $409,000. The one drag the listing can't fix is the school ratings (4/4/4), which argues for leaning even harder on price-per-foot and commute. No fireplace (resoFacts: 0) — minor at this price point, but it's why some 2004-vintage rivals show "cozier."

The fix

  1. Do not cut againRedirect the next $10K into payment, not price.

    You're already under builder-effective pricing. Advertise a seller-paid 2-1 buydown or closing-cost credit in the first line of the remarks and on your site — the builders' captive lenders sell payments, so match the weapon. A third cut invites lowballs below the new floor.

  2. Itemize what the builder doesn't include.

    Add a bulleted "versus new construction" block: full sod + sprinkler, blinds throughout, fence, garage door opener, no construction traffic, immediate close. Then name the number: "the nearest-size new build starts at $465K before options."

  3. Deepen the gallery.

    27 photos is thin for 3,015 sqft. Stage (virtually or lightly for real) the remaining empty rooms, add a floor plan page, and add one twilight exterior. Keep the disclosure.

  4. Manage by showings, not views.

    Traffic is proven; ask Joe for the showings-per-week count and buyer-agent feedback. If showings run 8+ with no offer by mid-September, the objection is in-person (schools, no fireplace, backing exposure) — meet it with the buydown, not the price.
