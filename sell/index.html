FAQ

Common questions

Do you buy land?
Yes. We review land, acreage, lots, and inherited land across Ellis County and Southern DFW.
Can you help if I'm facing foreclosure?
Often, yes. Reach out as early as you can. The more time we have, the more options there usually are.
Do I need to make repairs first?
No. We buy properties as-is, including homes that need repairs, cleanup, or updating.
Do you charge commissions?
No. There are no agent commissions when you sell directly to Recast Properties.
Do I need to clean out the property?
Not always. In many cases we can work with properties that still have items left behind.
Am I obligated to accept an offer?
No. You can review the offer and decide if it works for you. No pressure.
