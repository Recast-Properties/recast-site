Recast Analyst

Enter a listed property's address. The analyst pulls county records, live listing data, comps, and market statistics, writes the evaluation, and emails you the report link.

Reports take roughly 10–30 minutes and arrive by email; this page also updates if you leave it open. Runs are capped daily and reports go only to approved Recast addresses. Every figure is source-verified — anything unverifiable is labeled in the report.
